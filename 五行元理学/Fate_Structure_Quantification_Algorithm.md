## 三、原局计算  
## 3. Original Chart Calculation  

1. 提取八字四柱所有天干的数值  
2. 提取四柱地支中所有藏干的数值  
3. 全部数值求和，得 **原局总和**  

将原局总和与 55 比较：  
- 原局 ≥ 55 → 进入后天运化场  
- 原局 < 55 → 停留在先天生成场  

1. Extract the values of all heavenly stems from the four pillars of the Eight Characters.  
2. Extract the values of all hidden stems from the earthly branches of the four pillars.  
3. Sum all values to obtain the **Original Chart Total**.  

Compare the Original Chart Total with 55:  
- Original Chart ≥ 55 → enters the Postnatal Transformation Field  
- Original Chart < 55 → remains in the Prenatal Generation Field

## 四、大运计算  
## 4. Major Fortune Calculation  

### 4.1 定排运方向  
### 4.1 Determine Direction of Fortune Arrangement  

取年干赋值，按数值奇偶定年干类：  
- **偶年干**：甲(8)、丙(2)、戊(10)、庚(4)、壬(6)  
- **奇年干**：乙(3)、丁(7)、己(5)、辛(9)、癸(1)  

Take the value of the year stem and classify by parity:  
- **Even year stems**: Jia(8), Bing(2), Wu(10), Geng(4), Ren(6)  
- **Odd year stems**: Yi(3), Ding(7), Ji(5), Xin(9), Gui(1)  

排运方向（按年干奇偶 × 性别）：  
- 偶年干男命 → 顺排  
- 偶年干女命 → 逆排  
- 奇年干女命 → 顺排  
- 奇年干男命 → 逆排  

Fortune direction (based on year stem parity × gender):  
- Even year stem, male → Forward order  
- Even year stem, female → Reverse order  
- Odd year stem, female → Forward order  
- Odd year stem, male → Reverse order  

### 4.2 算起运岁数  
### 4.2 Calculate Starting Age for Fortune  

1. 二十四节气中，仅取十二个「节」：立春、惊蛰、清明、立夏、芒种、小暑、立秋、白露、寒露、立冬、大雪、小寒。「气」不算。  
2. 顺排者：从出生时刻往后数到下一个「节」  
3. 逆排者：从出生时刻往前数到上一个「节」  
4. 得到总天数（精确到时辰）  

换算规则（固定）：  
- **3 天 = 1 岁**  
- **1 天 = 4 个月**  
- **1 个时辰 = 10 天**  
- 1 小时 = 5 天  

公式：  
- 岁数 = 总天数 ÷ 3（商为整数岁）  
- 余数 1 天 → 加 4 个月  
- 余数 2 天 → 加 8 个月  
- 时辰零头 → 每时辰加 10 天  

例：  
- 数出 9 天 → 9÷3 = **3 岁**起运  
- 数出 7 天 → 7÷3 = 2 岁余 1 天 → **2 岁 4 个月**起运  
- 数出 4 天 3 时辰 → 1 岁 + 1 个月 + 10 天 → **1 岁 1 个月 10 天**起运  

1. Among the 24 solar terms, only take the 12 "Jie": Start of Spring, Awakening of Insects, Pure Brightness, Start of Summer, Grain in Ear, Minor Heat, Start of Autumn, White Dew, Cold Dew, Start of Winter, Major Snow, Minor Cold. The "Qi" are not counted.  
2. For forward arrangement: count from birth time forward to the next "Jie".  
3. For reverse arrangement: count from birth time backward to the previous "Jie".  
4. Obtain total days (accurate to the two-hour period).  

Conversion rules (fixed):  
- **3 days = 1 year**  
- **1 day = 4 months**  
- **1 two-hour period = 10 days**  
- 1 hour = 5 days  

Formula:  
- Age = total days ÷ 3 (quotient is integer years)  
- Remainder 1 day → add 4 months  
- Remainder 2 days → add 8 months  
- Fractional two-hour periods → each period adds 10 days  

Examples:  
- Count 9 days → 9÷3 = **3 years** starting age  
- Count 7 days → 7÷3 = 2 years remainder 1 day → **2 years 4 months** starting age  
- Count 4 days 3 two-hour periods → 1 year + 1 month + 10 days → **1 year 1 month 10 days** starting age  

### 4.3 排大运干支  
### 4.3 Arrange Major Fortune Stems and Branches  

以月柱为起点：  
- 顺排：按六十甲子顺序往下排  
- 逆排：按六十甲子逆序往上排  

每步大运固定 10 年，从起运岁数开始，每步顺延 10 岁。  

Start from the month pillar:  
- Forward order: arrange in the order of the Sixty Sexagenary Cycle downward.  
- Reverse order: arrange in reverse order of the Sixty Sexagenary Cycle upward.  

Each major fortune step is fixed at 10 years, starting from the starting age, extending 10 years per step.  

### 4.4 流年、流月、流日、流时叠加  
### 4.4 Superposition of Year, Month, Day, Hour Pillars  

在累计总和的基础上，流年、流月、流日、流时均按与大运相同的取数逻辑，逐级提取天干与地支藏干的数值并求和，依次向上叠加，得到对应时间粒度的累计总和。  

On the basis of the cumulative total, the year, month, day, and hour pillars follow the same value-extraction logic as the major fortune: extract the values of heavenly stems and hidden stems of earthly branches, sum them, and superimpose sequentially to obtain the cumulative total for the corresponding time granularity.  

### 4.5 计算每步累计总和  
### 4.5 Calculate Cumulative Total for Each Step  

每步大运，列出该运天干与地支藏干的数值，求和，得 **大运和**  

**累计总和 = 原局总和 + 大运和**  

For each major fortune step, list the values of its heavenly stem and hidden stems of its earthly branch, sum them to obtain the **Major Fortune Sum**.  

**Cumulative Total = Original Chart Total + Major Fortune Sum**

## 七、核心规律  
## 7. Core Principles  

1. **升为扩张，降为收敛**：斜率标定方向，不标定吉凶  
2. **数值越高，显化强度越大**：绝对值标定能级，不标定具体事件  
3. **同结构因相位差而分叉**：同八字者，大运顺逆不同导致峰值时间窗口错开  
4. **能量趋势可反映，具体情况无法反映**：框架标定势能，不标定选择

1. **Rise is expansion, fall is convergence**: Slope indicates direction, not good or bad fortune.  
2. **Higher value means greater manifestation intensity**: Absolute value indicates energy level, not specific events.  
3. **Same structure diverges due to phase difference**: People with identical Eight Characters have different fortune directions, causing peak time windows to stagger.  
4. **Energy trends can be reflected, but specific situations cannot**: The framework calibrates potential energy, not choices.
