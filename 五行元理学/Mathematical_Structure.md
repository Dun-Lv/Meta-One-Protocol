# Mathematical Structure

## Chinese Version

### 从河图洛书提取的数字结构

**木：** (6+4-2)+(1+9-7)=8+3  
**火：** (4+6-8)+(9+1-3)=2+7  
**金：** (2+8-6)+(7+3-1)=4+9  
**水：** (8+2-4)+(3+7-9)=6+1

这四个式子，每个都是偶数圈出一个数，奇数圈出一个数，俩数凑一对。  
四对凑完，正好把八个数字各用一遍，一个不多，一个不少。  
且过程加减不超出10。  
每个数字顺序不能乱，对着河图看有流动性。

**土：** 8+2=10  
4+6=10  
7-3+1=5  
9-7+3=5  
9-1-3=5  
10+5=15

你看这10个数字，1到10。  
只有三条链组成五，两条链组成10。其他链会显得过程超出10，或者说冗余。

8+3+2+7+4+9+6+1+5=45  
55-45=10（河图减洛书）

这些就是五元思想体系的深层数学结构。

在此结构中天干对应：  
甲=8，乙=3，丙=2，丁=7，戊=10，己=5，庚=4，辛=9，壬=6，癸=1。

## English Version

### Digital Structure Extracted from Hetu and Luoshu

**Wood:** (6+4-2)+(1+9-7)=8+3  
**Fire:** (4+6-8)+(9+1-3)=2+7  
**Metal:** (2+8-6)+(7+3-1)=4+9  
**Water:** (8+2-4)+(3+7-9)=6+1

Each of these four expressions takes one number from the even cycle and one from the odd cycle, pairing them together.  
After all four pairs are formed, each of the eight numbers is used exactly once — no more, no less.  
Throughout the entire process, addition and subtraction never exceed 10.  
The order of numbers follows the flow of the Hetu diagram.

**Earth:** 8+2=10  
4+6=10  
7-3+1=5  
9-7+3=5  
9-1-3=5  
10+5=15

Look at these ten numbers, from 1 to 10.  
Only three chains form five; two chains form ten. Any other chain would either exceed 10 or be redundant.

8+3+2+7+4+9+6+1+5=45  
55-45=10 (Hetu minus Luoshu)

These constitute the deep mathematical structure of the Five-Element Thought System.

Within this structure, the Heavenly Stems correspond as follows:  
Jia=8, Yi=3, Bing=2, Ding=7, Wu=10, Ji=5, Geng=4, Xin=9, Ren=6, Gui=1.
